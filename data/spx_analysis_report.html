General rule: any level below traded through and closed beyond
Every level in the tables above is labeled Support or Resistance based on today's close -- that label isn't fixed. If price opens or trades through a level marked Resistance and holds above it (doesn't just wick through), treat that same level as the new Support on any pullback -- the classic support/resistance 'polarity flip'. The same applies in reverse: a broken Support level tends to act as new Resistance on a bounce back up to it. Re-running the report after such a move will relabel every level automatically, since each level's Support/Resistance tag is recalculated from the latest close, not stored as a fixed designation.
